The full clinic management suite

One system, seamlessly integrated with every part of your clinic.

01

Queue & Flow

No patient should have to guess how long they'll wait, or interrupt reception to find out. Patients see their position and also know when a doctor's running behind, without having to ask reception.

02

Analytics & Trends

You shouldn't have to guess whether business is up or down this month. Revenue, patient volume, and automatic anomaly alerts. A fact, not just a feeling that Tuesday was busy.

03

Team & Doctors

One doctor's day off, another's late start, a holiday nobody flagged: easy to lose track of when it all lives in someone's head. Multiple doctors, staff roles, holidays, and closed dates managed from one place, not a notebook.

04

Patient Records

Paper records go missing. Memory fades. Neither should decide what a doctor knows about a patient. Every patient, every visit, searchable, plus automatic tracking of anyone who never showed up.

05

One System, Not a Stack of Registers

Running a clinic on paper means running it on whoever remembers where they wrote something down. No more flipping through pages to find a patient's history. Every visit, every note, one search away.

06

Consultation Billing, Handled

Manually written receipts are slow, easy to miscopy, and even easier to lose track of by the end of the day. Bill a consultation in seconds, with a printed receipt ready before the patient reaches the door.
