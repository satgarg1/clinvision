What's inside

Built around the three people
who decide if this actually gets used.

🧑‍💼

For your receptionist

A single screen to search, check in, and see the whole day's queue — walk-ins and phoned-in appointments merged fairly, replacing the register instead of adding to it.

🩺

For your doctor

One tap to broadcast "running late," "on a break," or "back in 10" — no staff interruption required, no public scoreboard.

📱

For your patients

An honest, updating status by SMS or WhatsApp — no app to install, and a private link they can share with whoever's picking them up.

📺

For the waiting room

A simple display screen showing who's being seen and who's next, in English or Hindi — visible proof that the wait is being managed.

📊

For the owner

A daily summary of footfall, no-shows, and delays — turning "it felt busy" into a specific fact, without flipping back through the register. (Weekly and monthly rollups are on the roadmap.)

🚀

For the future

Built to grow into ABHA-linked registration, capturing government digital-health incentives as they become standard.
