How it works

One shared queue. No new habits
for your staff or your patients.

01

Booked, same as always

A patient calls the clinic. Your receptionist enters the appointment — nothing changes for them.

02

A realistic time to leave home

The morning of, they get a message with a realistic window, based on how the doctor's day is actually running — not the printed slot time.

03

Arrival, exactly like today

They walk up and give their name. Your receptionist searches it, taps "arrived." That's the only new step, and it takes less time than the register.

04

Live position, honestly stated

The waiting room screen and their phone show where they stand — a range, not a false-precision countdown.

05

Delays, communicated automatically

If the doctor taps "running late," every waiting patient is updated at once. No one has to walk in and ask.

06

No-shows, handled quietly

Anyone who never arrives is flagged so their slot can go to a waiting walk-in, and closed out automatically at day's end.
