What's inside

Built around the people
who will use it.

Here's exactly what changes, one part of your day at a time.

🧑‍💼

For your receptionist

The questions that used to interrupt reception all day now answer themselves: one screen to search, check in, and see the whole queue, walk-ins and appointments merged fairly, replacing the register instead of adding another one.

🩺

For your doctor

One tap: "running late," "on a break," "back in 10," and every patient waiting for you knows, without anyone walking in to ask.

📱

For your patients

Somewhere in India, right now, someone is waiting, and no one's precisely told them for how long.

Every patient gets their own live link the moment they're booked: the real status, not a guess, sent straight to their phone by SMS or WhatsApp, so nobody has to wait blind or guess when it's time to leave home.

📺

For the waiting room

The waiting room gets the same visibility as everyone's phone: a screen showing who's being seen and who's next, in English or Hindi, so patients don't have to ask to know.

📊

For the owner

The register can tell you who came in. ClinVision tells you how the day actually went: footfall, no-shows, delays, summarized daily, no flipping back through pages. (Weekly and monthly rollups are on the roadmap.)

🚀

For the future

This isn't the finish line. ABHA-linked registration is coming, so the incentives becoming standard across Indian healthcare don't require an entirely different system in a year.
