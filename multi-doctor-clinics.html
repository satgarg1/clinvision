The problem

More doctors means more questions. All of them land on reception.

01

Doctors ask reception the same questions, over and over.

How many are waiting. Who's next. Is anyone running late.

02

Every doctor's queue lives in a different person's head.

Reception is the only place all of it comes together, if it comes together at all.

03

A doctor stepping away for ten minutes still has to be told to reception.

And told again the moment they're back.

04

The more doctors you add, the more reception becomes a call center.

Answering for people instead of helping the ones actually in front of them.

One shared system. Nobody has to ask anybody.
