Billing Audit

Every receipt number is generated by one atomic counter per clinic — issued once, never reused, never deleted (there's no way to remove an invoice anywhere in the app). A patient's own record and a receipt number aren't the same count on purpose: only a visit that actually reached the queue (arrived, not just booked) is billed, so a still-booked appointment or a no-show never gets a number at all.

Seen without an invoice

The one real gap worth watching: a patient is billed automatically the moment they arrive, unless the consulting doctor had no fee configured at that exact moment — that silently skips billing rather than guessing an amount.
