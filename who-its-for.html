One problem.
Every clinic feels it at a different scale.

The problem

Your patients have no way of knowing when to actually leave home, so they guess (arriving early out of caution, or late out of habit) and get it wrong the moment something genuinely unpredictable happens, like a doctor stepping away or running well behind schedule.

The fix

When your patients can see the real status themselves, they stop showing up all at once "just in case." Your waiting room clears as people time their arrival correctly, instead of staying full of people who guessed wrong.

Solo & small clinics

One doctor. Same problem.

One doctor, one or two staff, any specialty. With just one doctor, it's still your one staff member fielding the same question all day, in a waiting room too small to absorb everyone arriving "just in case."

Mid-size clinics & polyclinics

More doctors. The same guessing, multiplied.

2–10 doctors under one roof, one location, real walk-in and appointment volume. The same guessing now happens across every doctor at once. More patients, more of your reception's time lost to the same question, doctor after doctor.

Nursing homes & hospitals (OPD)

Same problem. A lot more people.

Smaller nursing homes up through larger multi-department hospitals. Your OPD has the same patients guessing at when to leave home, just multiplied across far more people, and less space to absorb the crowd it creates.
